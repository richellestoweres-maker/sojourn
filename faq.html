Frequently Asked Questions

Can't find your answer? Send us a message and we'll be glad to help.

Trip pricing and inclusions

What is included in the trip price?

Inclusions are different for every journey and are listed in full on each trip page under What's Included. Anything not listed there, such as items under Not Included, is not part of the trip price.

Are flights included?Owner to confirm

Each trip page states whether round trip airfare is included. If flights are not listed under What's Included, you will book your own and we'll share the recommended arrival and departure times.

Can I book my own flights?Owner to confirm

On trips where flights are not included, yes. Please wait until your reservation is confirmed before booking, and share your flight details with us so transfers can be arranged where they are included.

Are payment plans available?Owner to decide

Every trip lists its deposit and payment schedule on the trip page. If you'd like to discuss other payment options, you can choose that when you request your reservation and we'll follow up with you.

Traveling with the group

Do I need to travel with a group?

Our journeys are designed as small group experiences, so you'll share the itinerary with other guests. There is also free time throughout to explore on your own.

Can I travel alone?Owner to confirm

Yes, solo travelers are welcome. Choose a private room when you reserve. If you would prefer to share a room with another solo guest, mention it in your reservation request and we'll let you know what is possible.

How large are the groups?

Group sizes are kept small and are listed on each trip page.

Are trips suitable for all fitness levels?

Each trip page lists its important travel requirements, including the level of walking involved. If you have questions about a specific activity or need accommodations, contact us before reserving and we'll talk it through with you.

On the journey

Are the volunteer activities required?

No. Service experiences are a meaningful part of every journey, but taking part is always your choice.

Can I skip an activity?

Yes. Just let your trip host know. Activities marked Optional on the itinerary are always yours to join or skip. Skipping an included activity does not change the trip price.Owner to confirm

Will there be free time?

Yes. Every journey includes free time and at least one day designed for rest and relaxation.

What should I pack?

After your reservation is confirmed, you'll receive a packing guide tailored to the destination, season and activities on your journey.

What happens if an itinerary changes?

Itineraries are planned carefully, but weather, local conditions or partner availability can sometimes require changes. When that happens, we'll replace the activity with a comparable experience wherever possible and keep you informed.Owner to confirm

Who do I contact during the trip?Owner to decide

Your trip host travels with the group and is your first point of contact. Before departure, you'll receive their contact details along with an emergency contact number.

Insurance and cancellations

Is travel insurance required?Owner to decide

We strongly recommend travel insurance that covers trip cancellation, medical care and evacuation. Whether it is required will be stated in the booking terms for each journey.

What happens if I need to cancel?Owner to decide

Cancellation terms are shared before you pay a deposit and are summarized on each trip page. Please see our cancellation terms or contact us with any questions.
