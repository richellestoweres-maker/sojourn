Siera Stanton

Siera's background is in private aviation and elevated hospitality, where every journey is planned around the guest and nothing is left to chance.

Alongside that career is a lifelong love of history and archaeology, and a desire to share it. That passion inspired a different kind of travel company: one that combines thoughtful planning, authentic experiences, meaningful connections, and beautiful destinations. The same attention to detail that defines private travel now shapes every itinerary, every hotel choice, and every moment in between.
