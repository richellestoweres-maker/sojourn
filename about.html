Meet the founder

The Heart Behind Sojourn

My love for travel began long before I ever stepped foot on an airplane.

Growing up, some of my most cherished memories were made exploring the beauty of the American West. From hiking through the mountains of Utah and Colorado to camping beneath the stars in Arizona, Idaho, and Wyoming, my childhood was filled with adventure. I fell in love with the little towns tucked away along winding roads, the hidden caves, the untouched wilderness, and the unexpected discoveries waiting just off the beaten path.

Those experiences sparked something in me: an endless curiosity about the world and a desire to explore everything it had to offer.

As I grew older, I wasn't always certain what direction my life would take, but one thing never changed. I wanted to see the world. I was fascinated by maps, drawn to unfamiliar places, and captivated by the idea that there was always something new to discover.

That passion eventually led me to a career as a flight attendant, where I spent more than a decade traveling, meeting incredible people, and experiencing places I had once only dreamed of visiting.

It was a career that gave me so much, but somewhere along the way, I began to feel that something was missing.

I loved the destinations. I loved the connections. I loved the adventure. But I wanted something deeper. I wanted my experiences to have meaning beyond the places I visited. I wanted to make a difference.

After stepping away from aviation for a year, I found myself in a season of reflection and personal growth. It wasn't always easy. There were challenges, uncertainties, and plenty of moments when I questioned what was next. But through it all, one thing became increasingly clear.

I didn't just want to travel the world. I wanted to experience it with purpose.

I wanted to create opportunities to connect with different cultures, learn from the people who call these beautiful places home, give back to communities, and still embrace the joy and wonder of exploring somewhere new.

And that is the heart behind Sojourn.

A place where adventure meets intention. Where thoughtfully curated journeys offer more than beautiful destinations. Where we can explore, learn, give back, and take a little time to simply enjoy the world around us.

My hope is that every journey leaves you with unforgettable memories, meaningful connections, and perhaps even a new perspective on the world.

Because I believe the most beautiful part of travel isn't just discovering new places.
It's discovering what those places can awaken within us.

Siera Stanton
